When you first log in to Knowledge Base you'll land on the main dashboard — your central hub for everything from writing articles to reviewing performance data. The interface is designed to keep the most common tasks one click away, so this overview will help you get oriented and start working confidently right away.

## Dashboard sections

The left-hand sidebar is your primary navigation. It's organized into five main areas, each dedicated to a specific part of managing your knowledge base.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Articles" icon="file-lines">
    The **Articles** section is where all your content lives. Browse drafts, published articles, and archived content in one place. Use the search bar at the top to find any article by title or keyword, or filter by status, category, or author. Click **New Article** to open the editor and start writing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Categories" icon="folder-open">
    **Categories** lets you build the structure of your help center. Create top-level categories and nested subcategories, then drag and drop articles into place. The order you set here is exactly the order readers see in your published help center's navigation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    The **Analytics** section surfaces data about how readers interact with your content. See total views, average time on page, top-performing articles, failed search queries, and article feedback scores. Use this data to identify content gaps and prioritize what to write or update next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear">
    **Settings** is where you manage everything workspace-wide: your help center's branding (logo, colors, fonts), custom domain, team members and their roles, billing and plan details, and integrations with third-party tools. Changes to branding apply to your live help center immediately.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    The **Integrations** panel lets you connect Knowledge Base to tools your team already uses — including support platforms, chat widgets, Slack, and your own product via the REST API. Each integration has a dedicated setup guide accessible directly from the panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search Preview" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Use the **Search Preview** tool to test your help center's search from the same perspective as a customer. Type in common queries to verify that relevant articles surface correctly and to spot any gaps in your content or category labeling.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Top toolbar

The toolbar at the top of the dashboard gives you quick access to the most frequent actions:

- **New Article** — opens a blank article in the editor.
- **Search** — searches across all articles, categories, and settings in your workspace.
- **Notifications** — shows recent comments, review requests, and team activity.
- **Help** — links to this documentation and in-app support chat.

<Tip>
  Use the keyboard shortcut **⌘ K** (Mac) or **Ctrl K** (Windows/Linux) from anywhere in the dashboard to open the command palette. From there you can jump to any article, category, or settings page by typing a few characters — no mouse required. Power users find this the fastest way to navigate a large knowledge base.
</Tip>
