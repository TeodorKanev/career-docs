Knowledge Base is a centralized documentation platform built for teams who want to deliver great support without the chaos. Whether you're building an external help center for your customers or an internal wiki for your colleagues, Knowledge Base gives you the tools to write, organize, and publish content that people can actually find and use.

## What you can do

Knowledge Base is designed to grow with your team. Here's what you can accomplish from day one:

- **Author rich articles** — Write and format content using a powerful editor that supports text, images, code blocks, callouts, and more.
- **Organize with categories** — Group related articles into nested categories so readers can browse intuitively.
- **Search that just works** — Every published article is instantly indexed, so customers find answers without digging through menus.
- **Customize your help center** — Apply your brand colors, logo, and custom domain to make the help center feel like a natural extension of your product.
- **Collaborate with your team** — Invite teammates, assign roles, and review drafts before anything goes live.
- **Track what's working** — Use built-in analytics to see which articles get the most views, where readers drop off, and what searches come up empty.
- **Integrate with your tools** — Connect Knowledge Base to your support platform, chat widget, or app via the REST API.

<Note>
  The fastest way to get value from Knowledge Base is to start small. Pick your top five most-asked support questions, turn them into articles, and publish. You can always expand from there — and your customers will thank you immediately.
</Note>
