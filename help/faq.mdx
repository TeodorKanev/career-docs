Got questions? You're in the right place. Below you'll find answers to the questions our users ask most often, covering everything from article limits and custom domains to search, privacy, and billing. If your question isn't answered here, reach out to the support team from the chat widget in the bottom-right corner of any page.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many articles can I create?">
    The number of articles you can create depends on your plan. The **Starter** plan includes up to 50 published articles, the **Growth** plan supports up to 500, and the **Business** and **Enterprise** plans offer unlimited articles. Drafts do not count toward your published article limit. You can check your current usage at any time in **Settings > Usage**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a custom domain for my help center?">
    Yes. You can serve your public-facing knowledge base from a custom domain such as `help.yourcompany.com`. To set this up, go to **Settings > Custom Domain**, enter your desired subdomain, and follow the instructions to add a CNAME record with your DNS provider. SSL is provisioned automatically once your DNS changes propagate, which typically takes between a few minutes and 48 hours. Custom domains are available on the **Growth** plan and above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types can I attach to articles?">
    You can attach images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, SVG), documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV), and plain text files (TXT, MD) to any article. Individual file attachments are capped at **10 MB**. If you need to share larger files, we recommend hosting them externally and linking to them from within your article content. Executable files such as `.exe`, `.sh`, and `.bat` are not permitted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can customers leave feedback on articles?">
    Yes. Each article can display a simple thumbs-up / thumbs-down feedback widget at the bottom of the page. Visitors can rate an article as helpful or not helpful, and optionally leave a short comment. You can view aggregated feedback scores and individual comments in **Analytics > Article Feedback**. You can enable or disable feedback collection per article, or turn it off globally in **Settings > Feedback**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I export my knowledge base?">
    You can export your entire knowledge base as a ZIP archive containing Markdown files and attachments. Go to **Settings > Export**, choose your export format (Markdown or HTML), and click **Request Export**. You'll receive an email with a download link within a few minutes. Exports are available on all plans and include all published and draft articles, category structure, and metadata.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Yes. The **Free** plan lets you create up to 10 published articles, add one team member, and use the default `yourname.knowledgebase.io` subdomain at no cost — with no credit card required. The Free plan is ideal for trying out the platform or managing a very small knowledge base. When you're ready to grow, you can upgrade to a paid plan at any time from **Settings > Billing** and your existing content carries over automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does full-text search work?">
    Knowledge Base indexes the title, body, and tags of every published article. When a visitor types a query, the search engine performs full-text matching and ranks results by relevance using a combination of keyword frequency, recency, and article feedback scores. Search indexes are updated within a few minutes of publishing or updating an article. To improve discoverability, add relevant tags to your articles and make sure key terms appear in the article title. Internal-only articles are excluded from public search results.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I make some articles private (internal only)?">
    Yes. When editing any article, set its **Visibility** to **Internal** using the dropdown in the right-hand sidebar. Internal articles are visible only to logged-in team members in your Knowledge Base workspace and are never included in public search results, the public help center, or widget results. This lets you maintain a single knowledge base for both your customers and your internal team without managing two separate workspaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    To permanently delete your Knowledge Base account and all associated data, go to **Settings > Account > Danger Zone** and click **Delete Account**. You'll be asked to confirm by typing your workspace name. Deletion is irreversible — all articles, categories, media, analytics, and integrations are permanently removed within 24 hours. We recommend [exporting your content](#how-do-i-export-my-knowledge-base) before proceeding. If you're on a paid plan, deleting your account cancels your subscription immediately and you will not be charged in the next billing cycle.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
