Your Knowledge Base is most powerful when the right people can contribute to it — and when the wrong people cannot accidentally publish or delete content. The team management tools let you invite colleagues, assign granular roles, and keep full control over who can do what inside your workspace. Whether you are running a small team of two or coordinating dozens of contributors across departments, this guide covers everything you need to manage access confidently.

## Available Roles and Permissions

Each team member in your Knowledge Base workspace is assigned one of four roles. Roles apply workspace-wide — you cannot currently set per-category permissions.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to all workspace features, including creating and deleting articles, managing team members, changing workspace settings, and managing billing and subscription plans. There must always be at least one Admin in a workspace. |
| **Editor** | Create new articles, edit any existing article (including articles written by others), and publish or unpublish articles. Editors cannot access billing settings or remove team members. |
| **Writer** | Create new articles and edit their own drafts. Writers cannot publish articles — their work must be reviewed and published by an Editor or Admin. Writers cannot edit articles owned by other team members. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to all internal articles and drafts. Viewers cannot create, edit, or publish content. This role is useful for stakeholders who need visibility into the knowledge base without the ability to make changes. |

<Note>
  Viewer access only applies to your internal workspace view. Your public Help Center portal is accessible to all customers without any login — portal visibility is controlled by the article's **Published** status, not by team roles.
</Note>

## Inviting Team Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Click your workspace name in the top-left corner of the dashboard and select **Settings**. In the Settings menu, choose **Team & Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    On the Team page, click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner. A dialog box opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email address and choose a role">
    Type the invitee's work email address. Use the **Role** dropdown to assign them a role — Admin, Editor, Writer, or Viewer. Review the role descriptions above if you are unsure which to choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation and create their account (or log in if they already have one). The invitation expires after 7 days. You can resend it from the **Pending Invitations** section of the Team page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing a Team Member's Role

You can update a team member's role at any time without removing and re-inviting them.

1. Go to **Settings → Team & Permissions**.
2. Find the team member in the **Active Members** list.
3. Click the role badge next to their name (for example, **Writer**).
4. Select the new role from the dropdown.
5. The change takes effect immediately — no confirmation email is sent to the team member.

Keep in mind that downgrading an Editor to Writer will not unpublish any articles they have already published. It only prevents them from publishing new or edited articles going forward.

## Removing a Team Member

When someone leaves your team or no longer needs access, remove them from the workspace to revoke their login immediately.

1. Go to **Settings → Team & Permissions**.
2. Find the team member in the **Active Members** list.
3. Click the **⋯ Options** menu to the right of their name.
4. Select **Remove from Workspace**.
5. Confirm the removal in the dialog box.

The removed member loses access to the workspace dashboard instantly. Their existing articles remain published and intact — removing a team member does not affect live content.

<Note>
  Articles created by a team member retain their original author attribution even after that person is removed from the workspace. If you need to transfer ownership of an article — for example, to assign a new primary maintainer — open the article in the editor, click **⋯ More options**, and select **Transfer Ownership**. You can reassign ownership to any current Active Member. Article ownership affects the default notification recipient for comments and review requests on that article.
</Note>
