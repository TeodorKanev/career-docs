By the end of this guide you'll have a live, searchable help center complete with your first published article and a category structure ready to grow with your team. No technical background is required — if you can use a word processor, you can set up a Knowledge Base.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up & Create Your Account">
    Head to the Knowledge Base sign-up page and create your account using your work email address. After you confirm your email you'll land in the onboarding flow, which walks you through naming your workspace and choosing a subdomain (for example, `help.yourcompany.com`). The whole process takes under two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Knowledge Base">
    Once inside your dashboard, open **Settings → General** to add your company name, logo, and brand color. These details are applied to your public-facing help center immediately, so readers always see a polished, on-brand experience. You can also choose between a light or dark theme for your help center at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Article">
    Navigate to **Articles → New Article** and give your article a clear, question-style title — for example, "How do I reset my password?". Use the rich-text editor to write your answer, add images or code snippets as needed, and set a meta description so search engines can surface the content. When you're happy with the draft, click **Save** to keep it private or **Publish** to make it live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize into Categories">
    Open the **Categories** section from the left sidebar and click **New Category**. Give it a name that reflects a theme or product area — for example, "Account & Billing" — and drag your new article into it. Categories appear as sections in your help center's navigation, so a clear structure helps readers find what they need without using search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish Your Help Center">
    Go to **Settings → Publish** and toggle your help center to **Live**. Share the link with your team for a quick review, then announce it to your customers. From this point on, any article you publish is automatically added to the live help center — no re-deployment needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're live! Next, explore these guides to keep building out your help center.
